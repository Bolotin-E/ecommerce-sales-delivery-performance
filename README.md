# 📊 E-Commerce Sales & Delivery Performance

SQL and Tableau analytics project exploring sales performance, customer behavior, and delivery efficiency using the Brazilian Olist E-Commerce dataset.

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes e-commerce sales and logistics performance using SQL and Tableau.

The goal was to answer real-world business questions related to:

- Sales trends
- Revenue growth
- Customer spending behavior
- Delivery performance
- Regional revenue distribution
- Customer retention

The project combines SQL analytics with interactive Tableau visualizations.

---

## 📂 Dataset

**Dataset:** Brazilian Olist E-Commerce Dataset

The dataset contains information about:

- Orders
- Customers
- Payments
- Delivery dates
- Product purchases
- Customer locations

## 🔍 Business Questions

### Sales Performance

- What is the distribution of order statuses?
- How did order volume change over time?
- Which months generated the highest revenue?

### Customer Analytics

- Who are the top spending customers?
- What percentage of customers are repeat buyers?

### Revenue Analysis

- Which states generate the most revenue?
- What percentage of total revenue comes from each state?

### Delivery Performance

- What is the average delivery time?
- What percentage of deliveries are late?
- Which states have the highest late delivery rates?

## 💡 Key Insights

- Revenue is concentrated in a small number of customer states.
- A subset of customers generates a disproportionately large share of revenue.
- Delivery delays vary significantly by region.
- Monthly sales and revenue trends reveal clear seasonality patterns.
